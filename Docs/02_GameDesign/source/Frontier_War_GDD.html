Frontier War · Game Design Document01 · The game in one page
01 · The game in one page

Build. Drill. Expand. Overwhelm.

Frontier War is a classic sandbox RTS. You start with a Command Core and one Constructor. You build ore drills, factories and defences. Then you fill the map with tanks, mechs, aircraft, warships and finally a giant experimental machine.

The player fantasy

  • Start small, end huge. A few buggies at minute 1, hundreds of units and a walking fortress by minute 25.
  • Every domain matters. Land, air and sea each have real counters. Hover and amphibious units link them together.
  • Easy to learn, deep to master. One currency, clear unit roles and simple touch controls.
  • Looks modern. True-to-scale 3D battlefields with a crisp pixel filter, not low-res retro sprites.

Who it is for

Mobile players who love big RTS battles and want a deep single-player game with no internet, no energy timers and no pay-to-win.

47unit types across 5 groups
16buildings and defences
50story missions in 5 chapters
300+units on screen in normal battles
Platform and businessAndroid first (PC later is possible). Single-player only, fully offline. Premium price, or a free first chapter with a one-time unlock. No ads during play and no paid power.
Three design pillars 1. Scale: huge armies and smooth performance.
2. Clarity: you can always read what is happening, even at full zoom-out.
3. Freedom: many ways to win, whether that's a rush, turtling, air, navy or experimentals.

Frontier War · Game Design Document02 · Keep and improve
02 · What we keep, what we improve

The classic formula, made modern

Players love sandbox RTS games for their scale, their unit variety and how easy they are to pick up. Their usual weak spots are old-looking graphics, weak AI, units getting stuck, and short campaigns. We keep the first list and fix the second.

Keep (the core feel)

FeatureHow it works in Frontier War
Real base buildingConstructors place factories, drills, turrets and shields anywhere you have room.
Resource spotsOre Drills only go on ore deposits, so you must expand and fight for the map.
One simple currencyCredits. No wood, oil or power juggling.
Upgrades in placeDrills, factories and turrets upgrade where they stand.
Land, air, sea and hoverFour layers of movement, plus amphibious units that cross between them.
Experimental unitsHuge late-game machines that can turn a battle on their own.
Zoom all the way outThe whole map becomes clean icons, and you still give orders.
Fast, macro-focused playBig decisions matter more than fast clicking.

Pixel-filtered 3DTrue-to-scale world, hard shadows, smoke, wrecks and craters.

No traffic jamsFlow-field pathing and formations for big groups.
Drill choicesThe last drill upgrade is a 3-way choice: Fortified, Overdrive or Relay.
SalvageConstructors recycle wrecks into credits.
Infantry and coverCheap squads that hide in forests.
VeterancyUnits that survive get ranks and small bonuses.

Frontier War · Game Design Document03 · How a match plays
03 · How a match plays

The core loop

Every match follows the same easy-to-understand rhythm. Players choose how fast to move through it.

1BuildCommand Core trains Constructors
›
2DrillOre Drills on deposits earn credits
›
3ProduceFactories make units
›
4ExpandTake more deposits and defend them
›
5UpgradeTier 2 and Tier 3 tech
›
6OverwhelmExperimentals and the final push

A typical 20-minute skirmish

TimePhaseWhat the player does
0–3 minOpeningBuild 2–3 Constructors, drill the nearby deposits, scout with a buggy.
3–8 minEarly gameFirst Vehicle Plant, light tanks, grab contested deposits, first turret.
8–15 minMid gameCommand Core to Tier 2. Airfield, Mech Bay or Shipyard. Upgrade drills.
15–25 minLate gameTier 3, shields, artillery lines, an experimental unit, maybe a missile silo.
25+ minEndgameSeveral experimentals clash. Super-weapons and all-in attacks.

How to win and lose

Skirmish victoryDestroy every enemy building. Units alone do not keep a player alive, so hiding one tank does not stall the game.
Optional win rules (skirmish setup)Command Core kill: lose your Core and you lose.
Domination: hold 70% of deposits for 3 minutes.
Score: highest score when the timer ends.
CampaignEach mission states its own win and lose rules on the briefing screen. There are no surprise fail states.

Frontier War · Game Design Document04 · Economy
04 · Economy

One currency: credits

Everything costs credits. You earn them in three ways. Having more drills means more income, and more income means a bigger army.

SourceIncomeRule
Command Core+15 / secAlways pays. Losing it hurts badly.
Ore Drill+8 to +32 / secOnly on ore deposits. Upgradeable. This is your main income.
SalvageOne-timeA Constructor recycles a wreck for 25% of the unit's cost.

Ore Drill upgrade path

LevelCostIncomeHealthNotes
Drill L1600+8900Build on any free deposit
Drill L2+1,200+131,200Needs Command Core T2
Drill L3+3,200+202,000Needs Command Core T3

A destroyed drill leaves its deposit free. A Constructor can rebuild from L1.

Final choice at L3: pick one

Fortified
+2,500
Income stays +20. Health ×4, shield, self-repair and a small point-defence gun.
Best on the front line.
Overdrive
+6,000
Income +32. Health is halved and it shuts down for 30 s after heavy damage.
Best in safe areas.
Relay
+2,000
Income stays +20. Doubles build radius and vision, and reveals stealth and submarines nearby.
Best for forward bases.
Simple economy rules
  • No population cap stress: the cap is 300 units (setting: 100 to 600).
  • Costs are paid while building, so a low bank slows production instead of stopping it.
  • Cancelling refunds 100% of what was spent.
  • Income is shown as a big "+62/s" next to your credits.
Why this worksDeposits are fixed spots on the map, so the map itself creates fights. Every drill is a target, and every upgrade is a risk.

05 · Base building

Your base

Target look: Command Core with helipad (centre) under a Shield Dome, Vehicle Plant (top left), Mech Bay (left), Airfield with runway (top right), Shipyard on the lake (bottom left), three Ore Drills on deposits (right), and turrets and AA launchers along the roads. True to scale.

How building works

  • Constructors build everything. Tap a Constructor, pick a building, then tap the ground.
  • Several Constructors on one building build faster.
  • You can build anywhere that is free and flat. There is no build-radius limit, except for the Relay bonus that applies to drills.
  • Buildings show a ghost preview with range rings before you place them.
  • Constructors also repair and salvage.

Frontier War · Game Design Document14 · Combat rules
14 · Combat rules

Simple rules, rich battles

Fog of war
Black means never seen. Grey means seen before, showing the last-known buildings only. Units and buildings have a vision radius.
Terrain
Roads: +20% vehicle speed. Forest: infantry hidden and −40% damage taken, vehicles −30% speed. Hills: +25% vision and range. Rubble: slows tanks but not mechs.
Water layers
There is shallow water (hover and some mechs), deep water (ships, hover) and underwater (subs and the Leviathan). Lava blocks everything except air and the Colossus.
Veterancy
Kills give ranks: Veteran, Elite and Hero. Each rank gives +10% damage and +10% health, plus self-repair at Hero. Ranks show as chevrons.
Shields
Shields soak damage first and recharge after 5 s without hits. EMP drops them instantly. Shields show as a soft blue bubble.
Weather and night
A storm gives −40% vision and grounds jets. Night gives −30% vision, and gunfire briefly reveals units. These are campaign and skirmish options.
Wrecks
Destroyed vehicles leave wrecks for 3 minutes. Wrecks block small units a little and can be salvaged. Wrecks look darker so they never get confused with live units.
Stealth
The Stealth Bomber and submarines are invisible unless a detector is near: Sensor Tower, Relay Drill, Destroyer or Jammer Drone.
Friendly fire
Off by default for easier play. It is a "Realistic" option in skirmish settings.
Readability firstHealth bars, selection rings and orders always draw on top of smoke and explosions. At strategic zoom every unit becomes a clean icon (see page 18).

Frontier War · Game Design Document16 · Quality of life
16 · Quality-of-life features

Less tapping, more strategy

A phone has no mouse and keyboard, so we add smart helpers that keep the player focused on decisions.

Strategic zoom
Pinch all the way out to see the whole map. Units turn into icons by type (tank, mech, jet, ship, experimental) in team colours, and every order still works.
Tactical pause
Pause and give orders, then resume. Game speed can be ×0.5, ×1, ×1.5 or ×2.
Repeat build
Set any factory to loop its queue forever, with an optional credit floor such as "keep 1,000 in the bank".
Base blueprints
Save a group of buildings (for example "4 turrets + shield") and place the whole group with one tap. Constructors build it in order.
Idle alerts
A small badge shows idle Constructors and idle factories. Tap it to jump to them.
Auto-drill
Tell a Constructor "expand" and it builds drills on the nearest safe free deposits.
Smart retreat
A unit option sends units home to repair below 30% health.
Formations
Groups keep their shape. Fast units slow down to stay with the group when "keep together" is on.
Battle report
After each match: an income graph, a units-lost chart and your most valuable unit.
Tutorial approachThe first 5 campaign missions are the tutorial. Each teaches one thing with a short tip card. There are no long text walls. Hints can be turned off.

Frontier War · Game Design Document17 · Enemy AI
17 · Enemy AI

An opponent that feels like a player

This is single-player only, so the AI is the opponent. It must be smart, readable and fun to beat.

What the AI does

  • Builds an economy: takes deposits, upgrades drills and protects them.
  • Scouts first: it sends scouts before big attacks and reacts to what it sees.
  • Counters you: if you mass aircraft, it builds more anti-air.
  • Attacks in groups: it never feeds units one by one, retreats damaged groups, and flanks.
  • Defends: it pulls units back to a base under attack and builds turrets at contested drills.
  • Uses everything: transports, subs, support powers and experimentals.

Difficulty levels

LevelHow it changes
RecruitSlow decisions, no harassment, 70% income
RegularNormal decisions, fair income
VeteranFaster, counters you, multi-prong attacks
CommanderBest tactics, perfect counters
WarlordCommander + 150% income (for experts)

AI personalities (doctrines)

Blitz
Early rushes with light tanks and buggies.
Fortress
Turrets, shields and artillery. Slow and strong.
Sky Lord
Air-heavy, with bombers and gunships.
Admiral
Naval control and shore bombardment.

Big economy, racing to experimentals.
Balanced
Adapts to whatever you do.
Allies tooIn skirmish you can have AI allies. Give them simple orders with buttons: Attack here, Defend me, Expand, Send units.

True-to-scale 3D with a pixel filter

The locked look is a real game screenshot, not a painting: simple 3D models, a fixed angled camera, real-world scale, hard sun shadows and calm colours. A pixel filter on top gives the crisp, high-res pixel feel. Look and feel are inspired by modern PC RTS games such as Ebbtide.

Frontier War · Game Design Document22 · Technology
22 · Technology and performance

Big battles on a phone

Engine and structure

  • Engine: Unity 6 LTS with URP, Android first. Full technical design in Docs/03_TechnicalDesign.
  • Rendering: simple low-poly 3D with a fixed orthographic camera. The scene renders at 1/4 resolution and is scaled up with nearest-neighbour, which is the locked pixel filter. Rendering fewer pixels is also cheaper on phones.
  • Simulation separate from drawing: game logic runs at 20 ticks per second, and the drawing runs at 60 FPS.
  • Pathfinding: flow fields for groups and simple steering so units don't overlap. Separate maps for land, hover, sea and air.
  • Spatial grid: each unit only checks its neighbours, which keeps 500+ units fast.
  • Object pools: bullets, smoke and explosions are reused, not re-created.

Performance targets

TestTarget
Normal battle300 units, 60 FPS on mid-range phones
Big battle600 units, 45+ FPS
Stress test1,000 units, 30 FPS (strategic zoom)
Install sizeUnder 500 MB

Units are data files

Every unit and building is a data asset (a Unity ScriptableObject), so balancing needs no coding. A JSON version for player mods comes after launch. Example of the fields:

[unit] id = rail_tank name = Rail Tank factory = vehicle_plant tier = 2 cost = 1600 build_time = 22 health = 1400 armour = armoured speed = 1.1 vision = 9 [weapon_main] damage = 420 vs_armoured = 1.5 vs_infantry = 0.3 range = 11 reload = 3.2 projectile = rail_slug [art] model = units/land/rail_tank.glb turret = turret_main scale_m = 9.5 x 3.7

First prototype: done when…

  • You can build a Constructor, drill 3 deposits and build a Vehicle Plant on a real Android phone.
  • 200 tanks move across the map in groups without getting stuck.
  • A Light Tank, Rail Tank and SAM Truck clearly counter each other.
  • Strategic zoom shows icons and orders still work.
  • Units load from data files, and changing a number changes the game.
  • A simple AI builds, drills and attacks.

Don't build early

  • No campaign missions before skirmish is fun.
  • No final art before the camera and scale test passes on a phone.
  • No map editor before the data-file format is stable.
  • No more than 12 units until the counter system feels right.
