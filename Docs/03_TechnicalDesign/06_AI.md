# 06 · Enemy AI

The game is single-player only, so **the AI is the opponent.** The Rusted Warfare study found its AI is mostly a priority list plus an income multiplier for difficulty, which is why players call it weak. Ours is a small set of **managers** that each do one job well. The AI only issues the same `Commands` a player can (`00_Architecture`), so it can never cheat by touching the state.

---

## 1. Structure

```
AIPlayer (one per AI team, thinks every 10 ticks = 0.5 s, spread so teams don't think on the same tick)
 ├─ Blackboard      : what the AI knows (visible enemies, last-seen enemy base, threat map, my income, my army value)
 ├─ EconomyManager  : builders, drills, income targets
 ├─ BuildManager    : what buildings to place and where (follows a BuildOrder from the Doctrine)
 ├─ ProductionMgr   : what units to queue (army composition targets from the Doctrine + counters)
 ├─ ArmyManager     : groups units into Squads, picks missions (defend, attack, raid, harass, escort)
 ├─ ScoutManager    : keeps 1–2 scouts exploring, refreshes enemy info
 └─ DefenseManager  : reacts to attacks on my base and drills
```

## 2. Doctrine (personality) = a data asset

`AIDoctrineDef`: `name` · `buildOrder` (list of steps: "2 Constructor, Drill ×3, Vehicle Plant, Light Tank ×4…") · `compositionTargets` (for example Blitz: 60% light ground, 20% heavy, 20% AA) · `firstAttackTime` · `attackArmyValue` (the army size before attacking) · `turtleFactor` (0–1: how many turrets) · `expansionGreed` (0–1) · `techSpeed` (0–1) · `experimentalPreference` · `navalWeight` · `airWeight`.

Launch doctrines: **Blitz, Fortress, Sky Lord, Admiral, Engineer, Balanced** (GDD page 19).

## 3. Managers in detail

### EconomyManager
- Keeps `builders = 2 + drills/4` (max 8).
- **Wants drills on every safe free deposit.** "Safe" means threat map value < 20% of its local army.
- Upgrades drills when `credits > 2 × upgradeCost` and the drill is safe. Picks the final choice by threat: high threat gives Fortified, low threat gives Overdrive, and frontier positions give Relay.
- Salvages wrecks near the base when idle.

### BuildManager
- Follows the doctrine's `buildOrder` step by step. After the build order ends, it uses **rules**:
  - Factory count ≈ income / 60 (one more factory per 60 credits/s).
  - Turrets at drills under attack in the last 2 minutes (× `turtleFactor`).
  - Tier up when income > 80/s (T2) or > 160/s (T3), × `techSpeed`.
  - Experimental when T3 and income > 200/s and `experimentalPreference` passes a random roll every 60 s.
- **Placement:** a spiral search around the Core for a valid footprint. It keeps a 2-cell road gap between buildings so units never get trapped. Factories face their exit away from walls.

### ProductionManager
- Every 2 s, it compares the current army composition with `compositionTargets`, and queues the most-missing role at an idle factory.
- **Counter rule:** it looks at the enemy units seen in the last 3 minutes. If enemy air value > 25% of their army, it raises AA target to 30%. If enemy armour is high, it raises AntiArmour. This is the "AI reacts to you" feature.
- It respects the credit floor (keeps 300 credits for builders).

### ArmyManager
- Units belong to **Squads** (8–20 units). A new unit joins a forming squad at the rally point.
- Squad missions:

| Mission | When | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Defend | the base or a drill is under attack | Nearest squads attack-move there |
| Attack | squad value ≥ `attackArmyValue` and the enemy target is known | Attack-move to the target along a chosen lane |
| Raid | a fast squad (raiders) exists | Go for an enemy drill with low defence |
| Harass (air) | air units exist | Hit builders and artillery, avoid known AA |
| Hold | otherwise | Stay at the forward rally point |

- **Retreat rule:** if a squad loses more than 50% of its starting value and the local threat > its value, it retreats to base, repairs and joins a new squad.
- **Lane choice:** compute 3 paths to the target (direct, left and right, offset by the threat map). Pick the one with the least known threat, plus some randomness, so the AI flanks.
- **Target choice:** prefer, in order, enemy drills, then the army, then factories, then the Core (doctrine can change this).
- **Experimentals:** always escorted by a squad, never sent alone.

### ScoutManager
- Keeps one scout unit (or a Recon Drone) moving to the **least recently seen** region (the map split into 8×8 regions).
- Records the enemy base position, enemy army composition, and deposits.

### DefenseManager
- Listens to "my thing was damaged" events and adds threat to the threat map (an 8×8 region grid that decays 10% per 10 s).
- Pulls units back to defend if the threat at the base > 30% of the AI's army value.

## 4. Difficulty

| Level | Think interval | Income ×  | Reacts with counters | Micro (retreat, focus fire) | Mistakes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recruit | 2.0 s | 0.7 | No | No | Random delays, attacks early with small armies |
| Regular | 1.0 s | 1.0 | Slowly (every 3 min) | Retreat only | Some |
| Veteran | 0.5 s | 1.0 | Yes | Retreat + focus weak targets | Few |
| Commander | 0.5 s | 1.0 | Yes, fast | Full | None |
| Warlord | 0.5 s | 1.5 | Yes | Full | None |

Difficulty comes from **better decisions first** and from money only at Warlord.

## 5. AI allies (skirmish)

Allied AIs are the same AI with extra **player requests** (buttons): *Attack here* (sets the next attack target), *Defend me* (adds a request to defend the player's base), *Expand* (economy greed +0.3 for 3 min), *Send units* (gives the player one squad).

## 6. Tests and tuning tools

- **AI vs AI in Sandbox at ×4 speed:** each doctrine against Balanced, 10 games each. Log the win rate, the time of the first attack and the income curve. The aim is every doctrine winning 35–65%.
- **Debug overlay (F-key in editor):** shows the threat map, squads with mission labels and the current build-order step.
- **Never stuck test:** an AI alone on a map must reach T3 and build an experimental by minute 25.
